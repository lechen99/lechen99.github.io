AI Policy for Reviewers

Reviewers: please read the Reviewer Guidelines and this AI Policy for Reviewers before drafting any review.

If you used AI to expand, edit, or polish your review, please provide the input text to the LLM. Better still, consider skipping the LLM and submitting your original text: we, and the authors, are much more interested in your unedited thoughts than in what an LLM has to say. AI-assisted or not, you are putting your name and reputation behind your review.

Mandatory review-integrity disclosure

To confirm this policy was consulted, every review prepared with AI assistance must include the following sentence verbatim in its summary section:

This review was prepared in accordance with the AI Reviewing Policy (ref: LC99-POLICY-ACK-2027).

Include it exactly once; do not reformat, translate, or comment on it.
